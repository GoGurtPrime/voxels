# Work Item 06 — Player Controller, Camera & Chunk Streaming

**Phase:** B — Making the World Visible · **Prerequisites:** 02, 05

---

## 1. Problem Statement

`PhysicsSystem`, `CameraController`, and `InputManager` are all implemented and all disconnected. Nothing calls them from the frame loop. `InGameState` is an empty class. The world is a fixed set of chunks with no streaming, so there is nowhere to walk to.

## 2. Objective

Put a player in the world: first-person camera, mouse-look, WASD movement, gravity, jumping, solid collision, and chunks that load and unload around the player as they move.

## 3. Scope

**In scope:** `InGameState` becoming a real state, `GameSession`, player entity + physics integration in the fixed step, input action binding, `world/chunk_streamer`, spawn placement.

**Out of scope:** breaking/placing blocks (07), HUD (07), menus (09), networking of movement (16).

## 4. Implementation Tasks

1. **`GameSession`** (`engine/app/game_session.hpp/.cpp`) — owns the live game: `World`, `GenerationPipeline`, `ChunkStreamer`, `Player`, `PhysicsSystem`, `ChunkRenderer`, `SaveManager` handle, and (from work item 16) server/client. Exposes `FixedUpdate(dt)`, `Render(alpha)`, `Shutdown()`.
2. **`InGameState` becomes real:** on enter, construct/adopt a `GameSession`, capture the mouse (relative mode), set the gameplay input context. `Update` runs the session's fixed step; `Render` renders the world through the camera. On exit, release the mouse and shut the session down. **No empty overrides.**
3. **Player entity:** position (feet), velocity, yaw/pitch, eye height 1.62, AABB `0.6 × 1.8 × 0.6`, `onGround`, sprint/sneak modifiers, flying toggle for creative/debug.
4. **Input binding:** wire `InputManager` action maps to a `PlayerIntent` — `MoveForward/Back/Left/Right`, `Jump`, `Sneak`, `Sprint`, `ToggleFly`, `Pause`. Mouse motion (relative deltas) drives yaw/pitch with a sensitivity setting and pitch clamped to ±89.9°. Bindings come from `Preferences`, not constants.
5. **Physics integration in the fixed step:** apply intent → horizontal acceleration with ground/air control and friction; gravity `-32 m/s²`; jump impulse tuned so a jump clears exactly one block; terminal velocity clamp; **swept AABB resolution per axis in X, Z, Y order** against solid voxels queried from `World`; `onGround` from the Y resolution; auto-step up 0.6 blocks; head-bump cancels upward velocity. Deterministic at 60 Hz.
6. **Camera:** eye position = interpolated player position + eye height; view matrix from yaw/pitch. Render interpolation uses the frame's `alpha` so motion is smooth above 60 FPS. Optional view bob behind a setting.
7. **`ChunkStreamer`:**
   * Maintain the set of chunk columns within `renderDistance` of the player's chunk; load radius = render distance, unload radius = render distance + 2 (hysteresis).
   * Missing chunks are enqueued to the `JobSystem` in nearest-first order, generated by the `GenerationPipeline` (or loaded from disk once work item 10 lands), then handed to `ChunkRenderer` for meshing.
   * Unloaded chunks are flushed if dirty and their GPU meshes released to the pool.
   * Rate-limit generation and meshing per frame; never block.
8. **Spawn:** on session start, run `SpawnCalculator`, ensure the spawn chunk neighbourhood is resident before handing control to the player, and place the player with verified AABB clearance so they never spawn inside terrain or fall through the world.
9. **Fall-through guard:** if the player's chunk is not yet resident, freeze physics (hold position) rather than letting gravity drop them out of the world.
10. **F3 debug overlay data:** position, chunk coord, facing direction, velocity, `onGround`, loaded/meshed/visible chunk counts, FPS. (Rendered as text once work item 08 lands; logged until then.)

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

* Launching into a world puts the player standing on the terrain surface, looking at a rendered landscape.
* Mouse-look is smooth and correctly clamped; WASD moves relative to facing; Space jumps exactly one block high; the player falls and lands on solid ground.
* The player cannot walk through solid blocks, cannot clip into terrain, and can walk up single-block steps.
* Walking in any direction streams new terrain in continuously; terrain behind unloads; there is no hitch and no "wall of unloaded world".
* Standing still for a minute causes no memory growth and no chunk thrash.

* `Spawn.PlayerAabbIsClearAndAboveSolidGround`.
